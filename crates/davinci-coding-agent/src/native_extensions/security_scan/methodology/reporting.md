Native security methodology v1: reporting

The coordinator groups matching versioned root fingerprints within target or
supporting scope. A canonical finding preserves every assessed occurrence and
candidate link, including exact duplicate claim provenance. Its summary is one
identified representative occurrence, not a vote: confirmed evidence precedes
likely evidence, then severity, with discovery order breaking ties. Other occurrence
assessments remain independent. Failure policy checks every occurrence. Matching
fingerprints alone do not establish equivalence of differently worded root labels.

Inputs: closed candidate records, validated evidence, coverage and observed checks.
Output: concise remediation and caveats within the typed result contract.

The coordinator owns final classification, disposition closure, counts and sealing.
You cannot complete a scan by declaring success. Keep confirmed, likely, hardening
and deferred leads separate. Preserve every occurrence. Report only actual reviewed
scope and checks, explicit omissions and drift. An empty findings list means no
confirmed vulnerabilities found in reviewed scope, never that a repository is secure.

Do not reproduce credential values or terminal control characters. Do not edit,
publish, track, query accounts, switch providers, execute programs, or delegate.
Triage, tracking, policy authoring, fixing, fix verification, hardening portfolios
and disclosure drafting are separate inactive workflows requiring explicit requests.
