## Remaining product gaps

- **Photon beyond BMP**: clipboard BMP→PNG is ported. TS Photon still converts JPEG/WebP/GIF and applies EXIF orientation; those paths are not ported.
- **Extension shortcuts**: JS `registerShortcut` is recorded on load, resolved against reserved built-ins (TS skip/last-wins diagnostics), dispatched from InteractiveSession when no overlay is open, and listed by `/hotkeys`.
- **Raw `/dev/tty` OSC drain**: `drain_osc_tty` is independent of crossterm but still needs `PI_OSC_DRAIN_REPLY` / `PI_OSC_TTY`. Opening `/dev/tty` without those fixtures is not ported (no `nix` non-blocking read).
- **JS `RegExp` remainder**: session `re:` now covers anchors, alternation, classes, groups, braces, and `\d\w\s`. Flags, lookaround, and Unicode property classes are not ported.
